# Shipping and Currency

**How to edit this file:** one fact per line, as a list item under the heading it belongs to. Add a new rule as its own line, or a new `##` heading for a new topic. ## Shipping and prices outside Egypt

- **Shipping**: `settings/shipping` (public read, admin write) holds the Egypt boxes and the international boxes (one to three each, `box1`/`box2`/`box3`, each with max weight, L×W×H cm, and `feeEgp`), plus free-over, a scheduled promo, and a note. Zone fees, `baseFee`, and flat `internationalShippingFeeEgp` are removed (admin save deletes legacy fields; seed and checkout fall back to placeholder box rates until saved). Each region has one to three boxes (`box1`…`box3`; Egypt uses two: up to 1 kg = 115 EGP, up to 3 kg = 135 EGP, client rates Nov 2026). Customers never choose a box. `planDelivery()` (engine `shipping.ts`, shared by checkout and `priceStoreOrder`) weighs the bag with `parcelWeight()` and `assignShippingBox` picks the smallest box that fits. `shippingQuote` then takes the cheapest of the box fee, the Settings → Shipping promo, free-over, and live Free shipping offers (`reward.kind: 'freeShipping'`, minimum counted after item offers); Free shipping offers apply to Egypt orders only, so an order shipped abroad always pays its international box fee unless a Settings → Shipping rule makes it cheaper (tests in `shipping.spec.ts`). The storefront says the same: `offerTitleVisible(offer, visitorCountry)` (`@core/services/offers/offer-shop`, an unknown country counts as Egypt) keeps a Free shipping offer's title off the product page's offer lines and out of Shop's `?offer=` heading for a visitor outside Egypt; that Shop page still lists the offer's products, under the normal Shop title and with no subtitle (tested by `npm run test:cart`). `weightGrams` 0 is a real weight (socks never add to the parcel); a product with no weight counts as `ASSUMED_WEIGHT_GRAMS` (500 g) per unit, a last resort because every category has a default. Egypt orders heavier than every box are **delivery fee to be agreed**: the order is placed (InstaPay or Cash on Delivery only; Card is hidden and refused) with shipping line `kind: 'domestic-agreed'`, `priceEgp: 0`, `feeAgreed: false`, and `totalEgp` = items only. `orderStage()` returns `fee-to-be-agreed` (`isDeliveryFeeToBeAgreed()`), so no InstaPay box or payment request shows until staff agree the fee on WhatsApp and save it with the admin `setDeliveryFee` callable (Orders → Set Delivery Fee, changeable while open). That sets the shipping line, `feeAgreed: true`, `totalEgp`/`totalDisplay`, `deliveryFeeSetAt`/`deliveryFeeSetBy`, and sends the `delivery-fee-set` email the first time and `delivery-fee-changed` on each later change (claimed per save in `orderEmails/{orderId}_delivery-fee-changed_{deliveryFeeSetAt ms}`); `confirmCodOrder` refuses until then. Promotions and free shipping don't apply to these orders. Abroad heavier than every box is placed the same way (`international-agreed`, see **Orders from any country** below). Product weights: staff type one per product (product form, Weight (g)), or use **Products → Catalogue Tools → Shipping Weights** (admin callable `productWeights`: `preview`, `saveDefaults`, `fillEmpty`). That window edits each category's default (`categories/{id}.defaultWeightGrams`: grams, `null` = no default on purpose, missing = built-in), lists products with no weight (links to each), and Fill Empty Weights gives every product without a weight its default; a weight already set is never replaced. `defaultWeightGrams(categoryIds, name, saved)` in `catalog-taxonomy.ts` (both copies) resolves leaf, then parents, then the built-in `DEFAULT_WEIGHT_GRAMS_BY_CATEGORY` (client figures: t-shirts 250, shorts 250, hoodies 800, socks 0; our estimates until the client confirms: tracksuits 700, rackets 400, bags 900, balls 250, grips 50, bottles 200, supports 100, sets and bundles 500); any "jacket" by name is 800 g. The product form prefills the same default on create. Firestore can't query a missing field, so the "no weight" list lives in that window, not as a Products table filter. The server ignores any box on the client payload, prices with `shippingQuote`, and stores `boxId` plus `domestic-box` or `international-box` on the order. Checkout shows Subtotal, Shipping, and Total in the visitor's display currency. Edited in Settings → Shipping with an Egypt / Abroad region toggle; the storefront shows it as the shipping slide of `app-announcement-bar` (“from” = lowest Egypt box fee plus the lowest governorate extra, see below). `shippingSettingsResolver` on the main layout seeds `settings/shipping` through TransferState (`shipping-settings`) on SSR so Egypt first HTML includes the bar when currency is EGP. When the visitor's currency is not EGP, `bannerText` and `shippingLine` are empty. While `html[data-cur-pending]` is set, the announcement bar's shipping slide is `display: none` (and the whole bar when that is its only slide), so a foreign visitor does not see the EGP message before hydration and the bar does not reserve space for it. Egypt never sets that attribute, so the banner stays.
- **Outside Egypt prices**: `intlPricing/config` (markup percent, default 40, admin-only read) and `intlPricing/rates` (raw EGP rates, admin-only read) are written only by Cloud Functions. `settings/currency` is public read and functions-only write. It stores one combined `factor` per currency (`(1 + markup/100) * rate`) for every finite rate above 0 that open.er-api.com returns, except EGP. It has no markup field and no per-currency step. `refreshExchangeRates` (daily 04:00 Africa/Cairo, `open.er-api.com`) keeps the previous rate for any code that moved more than 50% on a scheduled run, and still saves the rest. A manual refresh accepts the jump and returns warnings. `setInternationalPricing` and `refreshExchangeRatesNow` are admin callables. There is no currency switcher; browsing follows the country the shopper told us when there is one (<KEY>-317, below). **Visitor location** comes from CDN/platform geo headers when present (App Hosting does not pass them today), then an IP lookup from `X-Forwarded-For` (order below) (`visitor-geo.ts`, `/api/visitor-location` for client-rendered routes). Express resolves geo once per HTML request, sets `x-atteya-visitor-country` on the incoming request (stripping any client-sent value first), and `serverCurrencyResolver` runs `CurrencyService.prepareForServerRender()` during SSR navigation (not `APP_INITIALIZER`, so `REQUEST` is available). Geo-priced storefront HTML (`/`, `product/**`, `category/**`, `brand/**`, `search`) uses `Cache-Control: private, no-store` so App Hosting CDN never caches visitor-specific prices. ISO country maps to display currency via CLDR (`scripts/generate-tz-currency.mjs` → `country-currency.ts`; functions copy `functions/src/intl/country-currency.generated.ts` + `currencyFromCountry()`). Egypt (`EG`) gets EGP (a visitor whose country can't be found: see the browsing order below); a country with no currency in the map or with one of `UNRELIABLE_CURRENCIES` (`LBP`, `SYP`, `YER`, `SDG`, `VES`, `ZWL`, `IRR`) uses USD. Non-production builds default unresolved geo (typical localhost/private IP) to `EG` / EGP via `applyDevLocalGeoFallback()` in `visitor-geo.ts`. Boot seeds transfer through `TransferState`. `?cur=` overrides one visit and is stripped, never stored. Prices round up in `toDisplayAmount` to a 1/2/5 step near 1.5% of the amount (whole units, or 3 decimals for `KWD`, `BHD`, `OMR`, and `JOD`). A foreign browser converts in `app-product-price` (`toDisplayAmount` + `formatMoney`). The Firestore read sits behind `import('./currency-loader')`. `OrderPricingSnapshot` (`src/app/core/services/orders/order-pricing.model.ts`) is the field contract: shipping country decides the order, and shipping stays a separate line with no product markup. International and Egypt shipping boxes live under Settings → Shipping (AS-46).
- **Browsing price rule (Youssef, Nov 14, 2026)**: anyone inside Egypt sees the Egypt price, anyone outside sees abroad prices. What a shopper pays is still decided by the delivery address at checkout; the browsing order below only decides what is shown before checkout.
- **Browsing order**: 1. CDN/platform geo headers; 2. `ipwho.is`, one retry only when it failed fast (not after a timeout), then `api.country.is`, all within a 2-second budget (`lookupCountryByIp()` in `visitor-geo.ts`); 3. the browser's time zone (`readTimeZone()` → `visitorBrowsingChoice()`): the zone's country from `tz-country.ts` (`Africa/Cairo` is Egypt), at that country's currency and abroad prices (USD when it has none or an unreliable one; a zone with no country, such as `Etc/UTC`, is USD); 4. no time zone either: EGP.
- **The country the shopper told us beats the guess (<KEY>-317)**: browsing prices (product pages, Shop, home rows, bag, search) use the first known of: `?cur=` (one visit); the remembered country in this browser; a signed-in shopper's saved address country; the guess above. Egypt is the Egypt price in EGP, anywhere else that country's currency at abroad prices.
- The remembered country is `localStorage` `atteya.browseCountry.v1` (`{ country, uid, savedAt }`, 30 days, never sent anywhere). It is set to the latest of: the delivery country picked at checkout (`CurrencyService.rememberCheckoutCountry()`, on each pick and at Place Order) and the country of an address saved in Account or saved by the first order (`rememberSavedAddressCountry()`).
- A choice made while signed in to another account is skipped (that account uses its own saved address); a guest's choice and a signed-out visit use any remembered country. Pure order: `preferredCountry()` and `browsingTarget()` in `browsing-currency.ts`, tested by `npm run test:cart`.
- `BrowsingAccountService` (`@core/services/currency/browsing-account.service.ts`, created by `MainLayoutComponent`, so store pages only, never `/admin`) reads `users/{uid}` once per sign-in and calls `CurrencyService.setAccount(uid, savedCountry)`; sign-out calls `setAccount(null, null)`, and a failed read passes `undefined` so the last-seen saved country is kept.
- The geo lookup (`/api/visitor-location`) runs only when `?cur=` and the shopper's country are both missing, so a shopper with a remembered or saved country triggers no IP lookup.
- `setAccount` keeps a hint in `localStorage` `atteya.savedCountry.v1` (`{ uid, country }`) so the next first paint knows the saved country before Firebase loads.
- No wrong price on first paint: the early script in `index.html` reads both keys (and `atteya-signed-in`), and sets `data-cur-pending` when the shopper's country differs from the server's `data-visitor-country`; `CurrencyService` hydrates the server's prices but leaves them hidden and not ready, then `#reconcile()` shows the right ones. Visitors with no remembered or saved country abroad paint exactly as before.
- Saving the address automatically: after a signed-in shopper places an order, `UserProfileService.saveAddressIfNone()` saves its delivery address as their saved address when they have none; an existing one is never replaced. What is charged still follows the delivery address on the server.
- The pure decision lives in `browsing-currency.ts` (`browsingChoice()` returns `{ country, currency }`, plus `currencyForCountry()` and `countryForTimeZone()`; no runtime imports) and is tested by `npm run test:cart`. `currencyFromCountry()` and `visitorBrowsingChoice()` in `currency-detect.ts` call it with the generated tables; `visitorBrowsingChoice()` loads `tz-country.ts` only when the IP country is unknown, and falls back to Egypt if that chunk can't load.
- `scripts/generate-tz-currency.mjs` writes `tz-country.ts` (zone → country, the same country `tz-currency.ts` takes its currency from) next to the other generated tables; it is in `.prettierignore` like them.
- IP lookups are cached in the server process per IP: a found country for 6 hours, a miss for 1 minute (so the Express middleware and SSR never both run every lookup for one page), and requests from one IP at the same time share one running lookup. The `ipwho.is` retry runs only after a network error or a 5xx answer. Each failed lookup logs `Visitor country lookup failed` with the service and the reason.
- Unknown country on the server: SSR renders EGP with prices hidden (`data-cur-pending` plus `data-cur-by-zone`, `currency-boot` transfer `code: null` with the currency factors), and the browser decides by its time zone straight away (no second `/api/visitor-location` call). The inline script in `index.html` removes `data-cur-pending` before first paint when `data-cur-by-zone` is set and the time zone is `Africa/Cairo`, so Egyptians see the Egypt price at once.
- Prerendered pages have no visitor at build time: they send no `currency-boot` decision, so the browser asks `/api/visitor-location` and then uses the time zone. Client-rendered routes do the same.
- A visitor placed by time zone gets that zone's country as `visitorCountry()`, so country rules (Free shipping titles, Cash on Delivery in the bag, the checkout country prefill) treat them like a visitor found by IP.
- On the time-zone path `CurrencyService` keeps `data-cur-pending` until `afterNextRender` has drawn the new currency, so the EGP price the server rendered never shows for a frame (ATS-321). IP-placed visitors remove it at once, as before.
- **Shipping abroad gets no markup (<KEY>-311)**: shipping prices abroad are set on their own in Settings → Shipping → Abroad, so the abroad markup applies to items only.
- `settings/currency` entries carry `shippingFactor` (the plain rate) next to `factor`, written by the same `buildPublicCurrencyDoc()`; someone reading both can work out the markup, which is acceptable.
- `shippingEntry(entry)` (engine `currency.ts`) is the one way to convert a shipping fee: checkout's Shipping row, `payInFor()` / `payIn.shipping` and the Card charge all use it. A doc without `shippingFactor` (written before <KEY>-311, until the next rate refresh or markup save) falls back to `factor` on both sides, so checkout and the server still agree.
- The browser's cached copy of `settings/currency` is `localStorage` `atteya.curDoc.v2` (v2 since <KEY>-311, so a cache without `shippingFactor` is never read).
- **Bank Transfer orders keep their rate (<KEY>-304)**: `pricing.payIn` of an order paid in another currency saves the `factor` it was worked out with and each line and the shipping in that currency.
- The `markupPercent` and the `rate` before the markup behind that factor go to the staff-only `orderStaffNotes/{orderId}.payInRate`, so the admin order page shows what the order comes to in pounds without reading live rates and shoppers never see the markup. See the `checkout-and-orders` skill.
- **Price factors in one transaction (<KEY>-233)**: `setInternationalPricing` (writes `intlPricing/config`, `settings/currency` and the audit log) and `refreshRates()` (writes `intlPricing/rates` and `settings/currency`) in `functions/src/intl/` read `intlPricing/rates` and `intlPricing/config` in the same `db.runTransaction` as their writes, so a markup save and a rate refresh at the same moment never mix inputs; the provider fetch stays outside the transaction, and a fetch older than the saved `providerUpdatedAt` only stamps `lastAttemptAt`, so it never rolls rates back.
- **The delivery country decides the currency at checkout (<KEY>-309)**: once the address is outside Egypt, checkout shows and prices everything in that country's currency (`CurrencyService.entryForDeliveryCountry()`), and the Bank Transfer `payIn` is worked out in it on the server (`currencyFromCountry(shipping.country)`), even when the visitor browsed in EGP. Egypt delivery shows EGP. See the `checkout-and-orders` skill for the order page and emails.


## Egypt delivery by governorate (<KEY>-306)

- Egypt delivery = the weight box fee + the governorate's extra fee. `settings/shipping.egyptGovernorates` is `{ [id]: { extraFeeEgp, active } }` (whole EGP, 0 to 1,000); a governorate with no entry counts as `DEFAULT_GOVERNORATE_SETTING` (10 EGP, delivered).
- The 27 governorates (id, English, Arabic, search aliases) live in the pricing engine's `governorates.ts` (`EGYPT_GOVERNORATES`), so checkout and the server share one list. Helpers there: `governorateById`, `checkGovernorate` (missing, unknown or switched off, plus the extra fee), `activeGovernorates`, `lowestGovernorateExtraFee`, `searchGovernorates` / `normalizeGovernorateText` (ignores case, spaces and hyphens; أ/إ/آ/ا, ة/ه and ى/ي match; a leading "ال" is optional; plain substring, no regex from input) and `governorateFromCity` (fills old typed cities).
- `shippingQuote` takes `governorateExtraEgp` (Egypt only) and the box candidate becomes `box.feeEgp + extra`; the quote returns `extraFeeEgp`. A promotion fee, free-over and Free shipping offers replace the whole delivery fee, so free shipping frees the extra too and a promotion above 0 never adds it. Orders whose delivery fee is agreed on WhatsApp get no extra.
- `shippingFromFee` (the announcement bar's "Shipping from …") is the lowest Egypt box fee plus the lowest extra of the governorates that are switched on.
- Checkout and Account → Delivery Address pick the governorate with `<app-governorate-input>`; a switched-off governorate is not listed. Until one is picked, checkout's Shipping line reads "Choose your governorate" and Place Order stops with "Choose your governorate.".
- Server: `parseCreateStoreOrderRequest` requires an Egypt `shipping.governorateId` from the list (else "Choose your governorate.") and sets `city` to its English name (a typed city is ignored, `region` is null). `priceStoreOrder` refuses a switched-off one with "We don't deliver to <Name> right now." (`egyptGovernorateExtraFee`) and adds its extra. The order saves `shipping.governorateId` and `shipping.city`, so admin Orders, Customers and the emails need no change.
- The saved address (`users/{uid}.address`) stores `governorateId` (Egypt) next to `city`; `firestore.rules` `validSavedAddress` allows it (null or a string up to 40). An old Egypt address without it is filled in from its city when it names a governorate or alias.
- Admin: Settings → Shipping → Egypt → **Delivery Fee by Governorate** lists all 27 with Extra Fee (EGP) and a "We deliver here" switch; Save Shipping writes the whole map and refuses a fee outside 0 to 1,000 or all governorates switched off. The `onShippingSettingsWritten` audit log records `egyptGovernorates`.


## Orders from any country (<KEY>-314)

- Checkout's Country box lists every country (`DELIVERY_COUNTRY_CODES` in the engine's `delivery-countries.ts`, names from `Intl.DisplayNames`, Egypt first) and is shown on the Egypt form too, set to Egypt by default; choosing another country switches to the abroad form, currency and prices.
- `settings/shipping.deliveryCountries` (ISO codes, admin write) is the list of countries abroad with a set shipping price; missing = `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_COUNTRIES` (the 16 countries checkout offered before). `parseDeliveryCountries()` cleans it (known codes only, never `EG`, no repeats).
- Edited in Settings → Shipping → Abroad → **Delivery Countries** (search box over every country, remove per row, saved with Save Shipping); the `onShippingSettingsWritten` audit log records `deliveryCountries`.
- `hasSetShippingPrice(settings, country)` decides: Egypt always; abroad only a listed country. `planDelivery(boxes, region, weight, setPrice)` returns `to-be-agreed` abroad for an unlisted country or a parcel over every abroad box; `too-heavy` no longer exists.
- The server (`planOrderDelivery()` in `order-pricing.server.ts`) decides from the saved settings, never from the client: listed + fits a box = `international-box`; otherwise `kind: 'international-agreed'`, `priceEgp: 0`, `feeAgreed: false`. `parseCreateStoreOrderRequest` refuses a code not in `DELIVERY_COUNTRY_CODES`.
- `international-agreed` orders are Bank Transfer only: Card is hidden at checkout and `createPaymobOrder` refuses with "Card isn't available when we agree delivery with you first." (`CARD_AGREED_ABROAD_MESSAGE`). Items are priced at abroad prices and `payIn` saves them in the shopper's currency with `shipping: 0`.
- Checkout shows Shipping "To be agreed", the total as "<items> + shipping", the note "We'll confirm delivery to {Country} and the shipping fee with you on WhatsApp. You pay once we agree." and the steps from `agreedBankTransferSteps()`.
- `isDeliveryFeeToBeAgreed()` covers both agreed kinds; `orderStage()` gives `fee-to-be-agreed` for a pending Bank Transfer abroad with the fee to be agreed, so no bank box or amount shows until staff set the fee.
- Staff see "Delivery to be confirmed · {Country}" (order page Delivery box, Orders list tag, `deliveryToConfirm()`), the Next step "Check that we can deliver to {Country}, agree the shipping fee with the customer on WhatsApp, then use Set Delivery Fee." and **Set Delivery Fee**; they can Cancel when delivery isn't possible.
- `setDeliveryFee` accepts `international-agreed` Bank Transfer orders: the fee is typed in EGP and `payInWithAgreedShipping()` (`pay-in.ts`) converts it at the plain rate (`payIn.shippingFactor`, else `orderStaffNotes.payInRate.rate`; refused with neither) and saves `payIn.shipping` and `payIn.amount` (items as saved + shipping).
- Emails: `bank-transfer-placed` for these orders says nothing to pay yet and that we'll contact them on WhatsApp; `delivery-fee-set` / `delivery-fee-changed` are also sent for Bank Transfer and show the shipping fee and total in the shopper's currency only, with the bank details; the staff email says "Confirm Delivery".
- Tests: `delivery-countries.test.ts` and `order-abroad-agreed.test.ts` in `functions/`.
